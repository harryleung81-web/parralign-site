Enterprise Engagements

Infrastructure & Implementation

Major national financial institution

The Situation

We owned compensation alignment. What we found went deeper — data silos creating inconsistency across audit, compliance, and compensation accuracy. The client knew something was wrong but couldn’t identify where it was coming from.

Our Approach

Instead of patching calculation scripts, we rebuilt the workflow logic from first principles, unified data expectations, and delivered an automated, audit-ready pipeline across every department through one of the most complex banking acquisitions in recent history.

The Outcome

What started as a localized repair became the firm's core operational intelligence engine — zero downtime, $741M in compensation managed accurately, and full audit-ready reporting across sales, ops, finance, HR, risk, compliance, and executive leadership — through one of the most complex banking acquisitions in recent history.

Diagnostic & Advisory

Regional banking group

The Situation

Engaged to assist with sales compensation. What we found was wider — key functionality the sales team was asking for didn’t exist, front-end reporting didn’t reflect back-end data, and plan logic had been built literally rather than with an understanding of the underlying compensation design.

Our Approach

We mapped the full landscape — front-end gaps, structural misalignments, compensation outputs misaligned with the intended plan design, and risks that without correction would compound over time. We delivered 24 pages of findings, corrections, and a prioritized build roadmap covering what would need to be rebuilt and why.

The Outcome

The engagement surfaced what a more targeted implementation would require to get right, giving leadership a clear picture of the gaps, the corrections needed, and a path to building it the way it should have been built from the start.
